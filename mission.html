Empowering human potential through thoughtful engineering.

CoAbility is a nonprofit initiative dedicated to improving the lives of children and individuals with disabilities through accessible, innovative, and personalized 3D-printed assistive devices.

Why CoAbility Exists

Our goal is to make everyday activities easier, more accessible, and more enjoyable through practical engineering and 3D printing.

The Challenge

Many everyday items—jackets, pens, utensils, bottle caps—require fine motor dexterity that can be painful or difficult for individuals with disabilities.

Traditional commercial assistive equipment is often mass-produced, cost-prohibitive, and inflexible, leaving individuals with generic tools that don't fit their unique anatomy or daily routines.

Our Solution

By combining 3D modeling and additive manufacturing, we can design and fabricate custom assistive tools tailored to specific physical needs.

From simple zipper attachments to ergonomic utensil handles, we create low-cost, durable solutions that restore daily confidence and promote independence.

What Guides Us

Three simple commitments behind every solution we design.

Personalized Fit

No two hands or mobility challenges are identical. 3D printing lets us tailor dimensions, grips, and angles to the individual rather than forcing someone to adapt to generic hardware.

Open & Accessible

Independence shouldn't carry medical markups. We aim to keep devices affordable and share open designs that schools, families, and maker communities can replicate anywhere.

Practical Impact

We focus on straightforward mechanical attachments that solve tangible everyday frustrations—making morning routines, classroom tasks, and hobbies easier to enjoy.
