Our Purpose & Conviction

Empowering human potential through thoughtful engineering.

CoAbility was founded on a simple belief: no one should have their independence limited by an everyday physical barrier that engineering and creativity can solve.

The Accessibility Challenge

Why traditional assistive equipment falls short

When someone experiences hand dexterity limitations, limb differences, arthritis, or motor coordination challenges, the commercial market rarely offers practical, personalized answers.

Commercial medical products are frequently mass-produced to rigid, standard dimensions. A tool that fits an adult hand is completely unusable for a growing 8-year-old child. Furthermore, the specialized medical supply chain often prices simple molded plastic tools at dozens or hundreds of dollars.

The Result: Many children and adults are forced to either struggle with daily pain, rely unnecessarily on caregivers, or give up on activities they love simply because the right physical interface does not exist in standard stores.
1

One-Size-Fits-All Design

Human anatomy and physical disabilities are endlessly varied. Mass-manufactured handles and grips fail to account for unique angles, grip radii, and muscle fatigue patterns.

2

Cost & Supply Barriers

Navigating medical insurance or specialty catalogs for assistive gadgets often involves excessive wait times, bureaucratic paperwork, and unjustifiable expense for basic pieces of hardware.

3

Sterile, Clinical Aesthetics

Most commercial aids look clinical and institutional. Children, in particular, deserve colorful, sleek, and friendly tools that celebrate personal expression rather than stigma.

Our Engineering Methodology

How we turn ideas into functional reality

By pairing modern digital 3D modeling with desktop additive manufacturing, CoAbility eliminates the barriers of traditional industrial tooling.

01

Listen & Understand

We work directly with individuals, parents, teachers, and occupational therapists to pinpoint the exact friction point in a daily routine.

Human-Centered Needs
02

Digital Precision
03

Rapid 3D Printing

Our Guiding Values

Principles that guide every prototype

Every file we model and every part we print is built with respect, equity, and engineering rigor.

Dignity & Autonomy

True accessibility isn't about doing things for someone; it's about providing the ergonomic leverage and assistive tools that allow individuals to do things independently on their own terms.

Open Collaboration

We believe assistive solutions should not be locked behind proprietary patents. We aim to contribute to an open maker culture where models can be shared with clinics, schools, and makers worldwide.

Practical Simplicity

The best engineering solution is often the simplest one. We strive for elegant, durable, mechanical attachments that don't require batteries, complicated setup, or brittle electronics to work reliably.
