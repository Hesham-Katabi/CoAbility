Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our emerging organization and process

How do individuals request an assistive device?

During this preliminary stage, you can submit your everyday challenge through our contact form. As our organization formalizes its intake workflow, we will launch an online design submission form.

Do you charge families for these devices?

CoAbility is founded as a nonprofit initiative. Our mission is to keep customized assistive tools accessible and eliminate financial barriers for families and individuals with disabilities.

What materials are used for 3D printing?

We primarily prototype with high-durability, non-toxic, and skin-safe polymers such as PETG, tough PLA+, and flexible TPU for ergonomic grips, ensuring parts are sturdy, lightweight, and washable.

How can makers and 3D printing hobbyists get involved?

We welcome volunteer CAD designers, mechanical engineers, and makers with FDM or resin printers. When a device is finalized, makers can help print and deliver devices to recipients in their local communities.
