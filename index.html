Innovation Meets Human Need

Why 3D printing changes the assistive landscape

Every person’s body, hand geometry, and daily environment are unique. Traditional manufacturing often leaves individuals behind with generic, expensive, or ill-fitting tools.

Tailored Personal Fit

Instead of forcing an individual to adapt to mass-produced hardware, 3D printing allows us to digitally tweak parametric dimensions to comfortably fit an exact grip, finger size, or range of motion.

Rapid Iterative Co-Design

We collaborate directly with children, adults, and occupational specialists. When a user tests a prototype, feedback can be integrated and reprinted within hours, accelerating practical impact.

Affordable & Accessible

Commercial medical equipment can carry hundreds or thousands of dollars in markups. Additive manufacturing brings material costs down to mere dollars, democratizing independence for all.

Transforming small barriers into everyday victories.

Simple daily tasks like zipping a jacket, opening a water bottle, or holding a pencil can pose frustrating hurdles for individuals with limited hand dexterity. CoAbility designs practical 3D-printed tools that restore ease, confidence, and independence.

  • ✓ Adaptive Zipper Pulls: Effortless whole-hand leverage for coats and backpacks.
  • ✓ Ergonomic Utensil Grips: Palm-stabilizing sleeves for dining cutlery and pens.
  • ✓ One-Handed Helpers: Countertop friction mounts for opening bottles and jars.
